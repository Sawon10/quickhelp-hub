This page is designed for people searching for support at home. Whether you are hiring for your own household or shortlisting workers for someone else, QuickHelp keeps the process calm, structured, and transparent.

How recruiters might use this

01

Post a need

Describe the job with timing, budget, area, and the kind of house help required.

02

Review suitable profiles

See who is available, who matches the schedule, and who prefers the same work arrangement.

03

Move into conversation

Once trust signals and basics line up, continue into profile review, contact, and booking.

What families care about most

  • Can this person work the needed shift?
  • Are they experienced in the exact type of home support needed?
  • Do they prefer live-in, part-time, or full-time arrangements?
  • How quickly can they start?
